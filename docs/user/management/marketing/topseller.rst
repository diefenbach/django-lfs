=========
Topseller
=========

This section describes the management interface for topsellers (``Marketing / Topseller``). LFS determines the
topsellers from the orders; here you can add products of your own choice to them, e.g. for a new shop without
orders yet.

Add topsellers
    In the section on the left (searchable by name, filtered by category) select the products and click ``Add to
    Topseller``.

Sort topsellers
    Drag a product in the section of the topsellers by its handle in the ``Sort`` column.

Remove topsellers
    Select the topsellers and click ``Remove from Topseller``.
